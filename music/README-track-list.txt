TOP 10 — Famous & Relaxing Chinese Music: Pipa, Guzheng, Dizi, Erhu
Downloaded for Amy, 2026-11-06. All tracks are MP3, tagged, and ready to use
as background music in the Baduanjin / Tai Chi dashboard.

THE TOP 10
1. Pipa 琵琶 — Yangchun Baixue 阳春白雪 (Bright Spring Snow) — 3:24
   One of the Ten Great Ancient Pieces. Light and bright.
2. Pipa 琵琶 — Han Gong Qiu Yue 汉宫秋月 (Autumn Moon over the Han Palace) — 6:38
   One of the Ten Great Ancient Pieces. Slow, calm, a little wistful.
3. Guzheng 古筝 — Gao Shan Liu Shui 高山流水 (High Mountain, Flowing Water) — 4:24
   The most famous guzheng piece of all — the "kindred spirit" (zhiyin) classic.
4. Guzheng + Pipa 古筝+琵琶 — Wuxia2 (PeriTune) — 4:16
   Gentle modern piece built on real guzheng and pipa. Made for backgrounds.
5. Bamboo flute (Dizi family) + Guzheng — Zen Session — 50:53
   Long relaxing guzheng & bamboo-flute session. Ideal under a full practice.
6. Xiao 箫 (Dizi family) — Ethereal Xiao Flute, Tranquil Chinese Melody — 1:54
   Short, soft flute interlude (MemoryMusic).
7. Erhu 二胡 — Er Quan Ying Yue 二泉映月 (Moon Reflected on Second Spring) — 4:28
   THE famous erhu piece, by the blind master Abing. Played by Zhang Peijian.
8. Erhu 二胡 — Jiang He Shui 江河水 (River Water) — 5:02
   Famous erhu classic, played by Zhang Peijian.
9. Gaohu 高胡 (Erhu family) — Yu Da Ba Jiao 雨打芭蕉 (Rain on Banana Leaves) — 4:15
   Beloved Cantonese classic, played by Zhang Peijian.
10. Ensemble — All The Tea In China (Shane Ivers) — 2:40
    Guzheng, erhu and flute together. Written as relaxing background music.

BONUS (famous, in the folder)
11. Pipa 琵琶 — Shi Mian Mai Fu 十面埋伏 (Ambush from Ten Sides) — 6:43
    The most famous pipa piece ever — but it's a battle scene, dramatic,
    not relaxing, so it's a bonus rather than in the Top 10.
12. Gaohu 高胡 — Lian Huan Kou 连环扣 — 3:45, Cantonese classic, Zhang Peijian.
